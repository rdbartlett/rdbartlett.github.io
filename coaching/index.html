Coaching

I’m a developmental coach for thoughtful, self-aware people trying to convert inner complexity into grounded action.

People come to me for enough space to hear themselves, a candid mirror, and some help turning what they already know into something they can actually do.

What brings people to me

Some recurring themes:

  • you need to have a difficult conversation, set a boundary or stand up for yourself;
  • you’re good at understanding everyone else’s perspective and sometimes lose contact with your own;
  • you’re building an unconventional organisation and the human dynamics have become confusing or exhausting;
  • you want to design a satisfying, unconventional life and talk with someone who has done it.

Working with me

I offer companionship while you look at what you would rather avoid. I’m also willing to interrupt, disagree and push back.

I love helping people through developmental transitions: those periods when an old way of organising your life has reached its limit and something new is trying to emerge.

I listen for the live developmental pattern beneath apparently separate stories and offer it as a hypothesis for us to test. I pay attention at three scales: what is happening inside you, what happens between you and other people, and the wider systems you are part of.

Depending on what helps, I might coach, mentor, offer strategic advice, share a personal example, rehearse a conversation or suggest an experiment. I’m especially useful when personal, relational and strategic material are tangled together.

An insight usually gives us something to do next: an experiment, a conversation or a decision. I sometimes share something from my own experience for you to accept, adapt or ignore. You remain the author of what happens next.

Book a session

Sessions are 60 minutes. Before we meet, I’ll ask a few questions so we can make good use of the time.

You can book a single session. If it seems useful to continue, we can arrange a short series after we meet.

Choose according to who is paying:

  • Organisation-funded — €240
  • Self-funded — €160
  • Supported — €80, by request

If your employer or organisation is paying, choose the organisation-funded rate. This helps me keep some of my calendar available to people with less financial capacity.

Supported places are limited and involve a short request form, but you won’t need to prove your hardship.
