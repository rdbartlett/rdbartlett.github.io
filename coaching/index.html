Coaching

I coach thoughtful, self-aware people who want to turn inner complexity into grounded action.

People come for space to hear themselves, a candid mirror, and help doing what they already know they need to do.

What brings people to me

Some recurring themes:

  • you need to have a difficult conversation, set a boundary or stand up for yourself;
  • you’re good at understanding everyone else’s perspective and sometimes lose contact with your own;
  • you’re building an unconventional organisation and its human dynamics have become confusing or exhausting;
  • you want an unconventional, satisfying life and guidance from someone who has built one.

Working with me

I offer companionship while you look at what you would rather avoid. I’ll also interrupt, disagree and push back.

I love helping people through developmental transitions: those periods when an old way of organising your life has reached its limit and something new is trying to emerge.

I listen for the pattern beneath apparently separate stories: what is happening inside you, between you and others, and in the wider systems you belong to. Depending on what helps, I might coach, mentor, offer strategic advice, rehearse a conversation or suggest an experiment.

Insight should lead somewhere: an experiment, a conversation or a decision. I may share from my own experience for you to accept, adapt or ignore. You remain the author of what happens next.
