The “secret sauce” — I’ll give it away

There is no magic prompt.

I built several small, standalone systems that are useful on their own:

Context is a compact map of my businesses and where the real sources live.

Projects remembers what matters now, whose move it is and when something needs my attention again.

Messages checks selected inboxes on demand and finds the conversations that need me.

Each module earns its keep independently.

Together, they create an emergent intelligence that is much more powerful than any module alone. A client’s WhatsApp question is no longer isolated text: the system knows who asked, which project it concerns and where to find the relevant budget, agreements and working methods.

It can connect the dots and draft a grounded answer for me to check.

The hard part is containing complexity. Agents naturally elaborate. Our job is to prune it down to an elegant simple system that’s perfectly adapted to your needs, without adding a bunch of pointless embellishments.

I add functionality only when real use proves it is needed.

Don’t copy my system as is. Take inspiration from it. Start with one problem, build the smallest elegant module that helps, use it, correct it, and only then add another piece.

What could your first module do?

Look for the work you repeatedly copy, check, chase, reformat or reconcile.
