I’ve spent the past twenty years experimenting with how people can live, work and grow together. These days I coach people 1:1, host groups at Casa Tilo and help mission-driven organisations collaborate.

About

Casa Tilo is a retreat centre and co-living space in the forests near Barcelona, where we treat hospitality as a sacred practice, convening the kind of people who change your life.

I’ve co-founded Microsolidarity (a network of mutual-aid communities), The Hum (organisational design for non-hierarchical teams), Enspiral (a legendary self-managing collective), and Loomio (collaborative decision-making software).

Coaching

I coach thoughtful, self-aware people who have a lot going on inside and want to turn some of that complexity into grounded action.

I offer a calm place to think, a candid mirror, and practical help with relationships, difficult conversations, leadership, and the work of building unconventional organisations or communities.
