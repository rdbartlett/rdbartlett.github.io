HOW TO ELIMINATE TEDIOUS DIGITAL WORK10

These robots want to help you eliminate all tedious digital work.

This is the skill I want to help you develop: identify what can be automated, delegate it safely, and build a personal system one useful job at a time.

01 · NOTICELearn to spot what can be automated.

Copying and pasting. Logistics. Checking. Chasing. Reformatting. Reconciling.

02 · SHAREShare your context with the robot.

Give it the relevant history, examples and source material.

03 · CLARIFYGet clear on your intention.

Explain what should happen, how it should work, the boundaries, and what a good result looks like.

04 · SPEAKUse normal conversational language.

The only technical skill you need is the ability to express your intentions precisely and give feedback when your expectations are not met.

05 · ITERATEStart with one useful slice, then iterate.

Use it in real life. Notice what is missing. Embellish it gradually.

Start read-only or draft-only. Keep consequential decisions human until the job is stable.

The aim is much higher digital productivity with far less digital tedium and mental load—leaving more attention for discernment, personal development, relationships, creativity, fun and higher-leverage work.
